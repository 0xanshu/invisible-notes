👻 Hidden from capture

Yours to see. Invisible to everyone else.

Built with a real OS-level capture-exclusion flag — not a hack. The notes simply don't exist in any recording or shared feed.

🫥

Invisible to capture

Uses content protection so Zoom, Meet, Teams, QuickTime, OBS and macOS recording all skip the notes entirely.

👻

Click-through mode

Flip a note to ghost mode and your clicks pass straight through to the app behind it — the note stays visible.

🪟

Always on top

Floats above everything, including fullscreen apps and other displays, so your notes are always in reach.

🎨

Make it yours

Six colors, adjustable opacity and text size per note. Drag, resize, and stack as many as you like.

💾

Auto-saved

Text, position, size and style persist automatically and reappear exactly where you left them.

⌨️

Menu-bar & shortcuts

Lives quietly in the menu bar / tray. New note, hide-all, and click-through all have global hotkeys.

Up and running in seconds

No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Download, open, start typing.

Install

Grab the build for your OS and open it. On macOS, right-click → Open the first time.

Jot a note

A note appears near your cursor. Press ⌘⇧N / Ctrl+Shift+N for more.

Share with confidence

Start your call or recording — your notes stay on screen for you, and vanish for everyone else.
